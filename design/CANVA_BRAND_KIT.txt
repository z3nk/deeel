====================================================================

Design for "Deeelicious", a student app that finds the best lunch (main + snack + drink) at six shops near UCL Bloomsbury and scores every basket 0-100 with the "Deeel score". The style is "Snack hype": loud, bold, sticker-heavy and fun, built on one deep green brand colour with sunshine yellow, electric violet and cyan accents on an off-white background. Headlines in Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, body text in Figtree. Stickers have a thick dark outline, a hard offset shadow with no blur, and a slight tilt. Lead with real numbers (prices, minutes, scores). No pink, no red except for allergy warnings, no gradients, no stock photos of people, no supermarket logos, no UCL branding.


====================================================================
1. WHAT DEEELICIOUS IS
====================================================================

- One line: Deeelicious finds your best lunch near UCL Bloomsbury: the best main + snack + drink, meal deal against buying separately, ranked out of 100.
- Who uses it: students, on a phone, standing in a shop, deciding in under 30 seconds.
- The shops: Tesco, Sainsbury's, Waitrose, M&S, Lidl and Pret near UCL Bloomsbury.
- The Deeel score (0-100) weighs: value 30%, fillingness 25%, ingredient quality 25%, nutrition balance 10%, deal saving 10%.
- The data comes from shelf photos students take in the shops. Students earn credits for photos, paid only once the team approves them.
- Allergy and diet filters use the printed label text. Anything the label doesn't show counts as unsafe.


====================================================================
2. NAME AND SPELLING
====================================================================

- The name is Deeelicious: three Es, one word, capital D.
- Never write it "Delicious", "Deelicious" or "DEEELICIOUS" (all caps is fine only as a deliberate headline graphic, never in sentences).
- The score is the "Deeel score": capital D, lowercase "score".


====================================================================
3. THE TWO MODES: HYPE AND SERIOUS
====================================================================

Decide which mode every element is in before styling it.

HYPE MODE (the default for marketing)
- For headlines, prices, savings, the Deeel score, credits and rewards.
- Use the display font, the accent colours, chunky stickers with a 2px dark outline, a hard offset shadow and a slight tilt (about 3 to 6 degrees).

SERIOUS MODE
- For allergy info, "sample data" labels and the Open Food Facts credit.
- Use the body font (Figtree) only, plain text colours, thin hairline borders.
- No stickers, no accent colours, no tilt, no jokes.
- Never put hype styling on a safety message, even when it sits inside a hype layout.


====================================================================
4. COLOUR PALETTE
====================================================================

Add these to the Canva Brand Kit.

BRAND
- Deeel Green (primary) .............. #0F7B5F
  The brand anchor. Main buttons, CTAs, "Best match" badges, key shapes. Put white text on it.
- Green Tint ......................... #E7F2EE
  Soft green panel or card background. Dark text on it.

HYPE ACCENTS (marketing and fun bits only)
- Sunshine Yellow .................... #FFD23F
  Savings stickers ("Meal deal saves £1.70"), credits, attention blocks. DARK TEXT ONLY, never white.
- Electric Violet .................... #7B2FF7
  Deeel score graphics, stickers, poster blocks. White text on it.
- Cyan ............................... #00C2FF
  Stickers and marketing blocks. DARK TEXT ONLY, never white.

NEUTRALS
- Off-white (page background) ........ #F7F7F4
- White (cards, surfaces) ............ #FFFFFF
- Ink (main text, outlines, shadows) . #1F2328
- Muted grey (secondary text) ........ #6B7280  (only on white or off-white)
- Hairline border .................... #E3E3DE  (decorative lines only)
- Strong outline ..................... #8A8F98

DEEEL SCORE BANDS (score badge colour, always with the number on it)
- Score 70+ .......................... #15803D  white text
- Score 50-69 ........................ #B45309  white text
- Score under 50 ..................... #64748B  white text

SAFETY (serious mode only)
- Allergy red (fill) ................. #DC2626  white text
- Allergy red (text) ................. #B91C1C
- Allergy warning background ......... #FEF2F2
- Sample-data grey text .............. #475569 on #F1F5F9

COLOUR RULES
- The brand palette has no pinks or reds. Red appears ONLY for allergy warnings and errors, so it always means something.
- Yellow and cyan always take dark ink text (#1F2328). Green and violet take white.
- Never use colour as the only signal: a score badge shows the number, a rank shows the number, a warning shows words.
- Flat colour only. No gradients, no glows, no neon effects.
- Light backgrounds only. There is no dark theme.
- Good combinations: green + yellow, violet + yellow, cyan + ink, off-white background with one or two accent blocks. Don't use all three accents at equal weight in one design; pick one lead accent.


====================================================================
5. TYPOGRAPHY
====================================================================

Two fonts, both free Google Fonts (both are in Canva's font library; upload them to the Brand Kit if not).

DISPLAY: Bricolage Grotesque (weights 600 to 800)
- For headlines, big prices and the big score numbers.
- Headlines: ExtraBold (800), tight letter spacing (about -2%), line height about 1.0.

BODY: Figtree (Regular 400, SemiBold 600, Bold 700)
- For everything else: subheads, body text, labels, buttons, captions, and ALL safety text.

TYPE SCALE (app sizes; scale up proportionally for posters and slides)
- Marketing headline ... Bricolage Grotesque 800, 56px, line height 1.0, -2% spacing
- Big score number ..... Bricolage Grotesque 800, 40px
- Screen title ......... Bricolage Grotesque 800, 28px
- Section heading ...... Bricolage Grotesque 700, 20px
- Big price ............ Bricolage Grotesque 700, 28px
- Card heading ......... Figtree 700, 16px
- Body ................. Figtree 400, 14px
- Body emphasis ........ Figtree 600, 14px
- Label / button / chip  Figtree 600, 13px
- Caption / credit ..... Figtree 400, 12px
- Small badge .......... Figtree 600, 11px (the smallest size, never smaller)

TYPE RULES
- Sentence case everywhere ("Find my lunch", not "Find My Lunch").
- Prices and scores should line up (tabular figures) where they sit in columns.
- Headlines in ink (#1F2328) or Deeel Green on light backgrounds, white on green or violet, ink on yellow or cyan.


====================================================================
6. SHAPES, SPACING AND EFFECTS
====================================================================

CORNERS
- Pills, chips, badges, score badge: fully rounded (pill shape).
- Buttons and boxes: 8px radius.
- Cards and panels: 12px radius.
- Small badges and thumbnails: 6px radius.

SPACING
- Use a 4-point grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32px.
- Marketing layouts use generous margins (32px at app scale; scale up for print).

EFFECTS
- Sticker look (hype mode only): solid fill, 2px outline in ink #1F2328, hard drop shadow offset 3px right and 3px down in #1F2328 with NO blur, tilted 3 to 6 degrees.
- Cards: thin 1px border in #E3E3DE, no shadow. The featured card has a 2px Deeel Green border on the Green Tint background.
- At most two levels of depth. No soft blurry shadows, no glassmorphism, no 3D.


====================================================================
7. LOGO
====================================================================

- A teammate is designing the logo. Don't let Canva AI generate or approximate one.
- Until it's ready, use the wordmark "Deeelicious" typed in Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold (800), in ink or Deeel Green on off-white/white, or in white on Deeel Green.
- Leave a logo slot in every layout.
- Clear space: at least 32px (at app scale) on all sides in marketing.
- Minimum height: 20px on screen.
- Place it only on off-white, white, Deeel Green or Sunshine Yellow. Never over photos or patterns.


====================================================================
8. IMAGERY AND ICONS
====================================================================

- Use real shelf photos taken by the team in the shops. Avoid stock food photos.
- No photos of people's faces.
- Illustration is fine for small accents only (doodles, arrows, sparkles around stickers).
- Icons: simple line icons, ink colour, consistent stroke. Useful ones: walking minutes, hot/cold, meal deal, loyalty card, camera, credits/coins, map pin, barcode, warning.
- App screenshots go inside a phone frame (and a laptop frame for desktop shots).


====================================================================
9. VOICE AND COPY
====================================================================

- Lead with numbers. Plain words. Talk to the reader as "you".
- Playful about lunch. Never playful about allergies, health, or guaranteed prices.

WRITE THIS / NOT THIS
- "£3.85 at Tesco · 4 min" ............ not "We found a great option for you!"
- "Meal deal saves £1.70" ............. not "Amazing savings!!"
- "Nothing under £4 within 5 min. Try 10 min?" ... not "No results found."
- "+10 credits once approved" ......... not "You've earned 10 credits!"
- "Best basket" ....................... not "optimised result"

HEADLINES YOU CAN USE
- "Your meal deal, but with maths."
- "£3.85 or £5.55? We checked the shelf."
- "Lunch ranked out of 100. Meet the Deeel score."
- "Six shops. Ten minutes. One best lunch."
- "Snap a shelf, earn credits."
- "Allergy-aware lunch finding — and we'll always tell you to check the pack."

CTA BUTTON TEXT
- "Find my lunch"


====================================================================
10. HARD RULES (every asset)
====================================================================

1. No supermarket logos in marketing. Shop names in plain text are fine.
2. No UCL or Students' Union logos, crests, colours or names used as branding, and nothing that implies a partnership. "Near UCL Bloomsbury" as a location is fine.
3. Never claim "always the cheapest", "allergen-safe", "allergy-proof" or anything similar.
4. Any mention of allergens carries the warning: "⚠️ computer-read — check the pack". Serious mode, never shrunk, faded or hidden.
5. Sponsor and rewards content stays visibly separate from rankings, with the line "Sponsors never affect ranking".
6. Credits are only "earned" once approved. Say "once approved".
7. If nutrition data is shown, credit it in small text: "Some nutrition data from Open Food Facts".
8. Contrast: body text needs at least 4.5:1 against its background, large text and icons at least 3:1. Stick to the colour pairings in section 4.


====================================================================
11. FORMATS TO MAKE
====================================================================

PITCH DECK (16:9, 1920x1080)
- Slides: Title · Problem · Demo screenshot · How the Deeel score works · Data and trust · Team.
- Title slide: off-white or Deeel Green background, big wordmark, one headline, one accent sticker.
- Deeel score slide: big score numeral in a violet or green badge, with the five weights (value 30, fillingness 25, ingredient quality 25, nutrition balance 10, deal saving 10) as a simple bar or list.
- Data and trust slide: serious mode. Shelf photos, label-only allergy data, "unknown counts as unsafe", team review before credits pay out, Open Food Facts credit.
- One idea per slide, big numbers, lots of off-white space.

INSTAGRAM POST (1080x1080) AND STORY (1080x1920)
- "Meal deal vs separate": two prices side by side, the winner in a yellow savings sticker.
- "Deeel of the day": one lunch result card (shop, three items, price, walk time, Deeel score badge).
- "New shop photographed".
- "Earn credits": "Snap a shelf, earn credits." with a camera icon. Include "once approved".
- Rewards / sponsor: "Spend your credits on…" with a clearly separate sponsor logo slot and "Sponsors never affect ranking".

A3 CAMPUS POSTER (297x420mm)
- One huge headline, one example result card or price comparison, QR code to the live app, the wordmark, "Find my lunch".

LANDING PAGE HERO (desktop and mobile)
- Headline, one-line sub, phone screenshot, "Find my lunch" button in Deeel Green with white text.
